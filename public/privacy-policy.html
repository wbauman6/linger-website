Privacy Policy

Last updated: April 30, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how Bauman Apps LLC ("we," "us," "our") collects, uses, and protects your information when you use Linger (the "App"). By using Linger, you agree to this policy.

1. Information We Collect

Information you provide

  • Account info: name, email, profile photo.
  • Content: photos, videos, captions, and memory titles you upload.
  • Invitations: contact info you choose to share when inviting friends to a memory.

Automatically collected

  • Device info, app version, crash logs, and basic analytics to diagnose issues.

2. How We Use Your Information

  • To operate the App and deliver memories to the people you invite.
  • To provide customer support and respond to your requests.
  • To improve reliability, performance, and security.
  • To process payments for Linger Plus via Apple.

We do not sell your data. We do not use your photos or videos to train AI models.

3. Privacy of Memories

Memories are private by default. Only people you invite to a specific memory can see its content. Linger has no public feed.

4. Sharing

We share information only with service providers who help us run the App (e.g., cloud storage, crash reporting), under contracts that limit their use of the data. We may disclose information if required by law.

5. Data Retention & Deletion

You can delete a photo, video, or memory at any time in the App. To delete your account and all associated data, email support@thelingerapp.com.

6. Children’s Privacy

Linger is not directed to children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13.

7. Your Rights

Depending on where you live, you may have rights to access, correct, or delete your personal data. Email us at support@thelingerapp.com to make a request.

8. Security

We use industry-standard measures to protect your data in transit and at rest. No system is perfectly secure, but we work hard to keep your memories safe.

9. Changes to This Policy

We may update this policy from time to time. Material changes will be reflected in the "Last updated" date above and, where appropriate, surfaced in the App.
